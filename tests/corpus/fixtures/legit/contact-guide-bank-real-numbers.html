How to Contact Wells Fargo by Phone

For everyday banking questions, call Wells Fargo customer service at 1-800-869-3557, available 24 hours a day.

For card questions, the Wells Fargo phone number for credit card account management is 1-800-642-4720.

To report fraud on a business account, contact Wells Fargo at 1-800-225-5935.

Banking with Bank of America instead? Bank of America customer service is 800.432.1000, and Wells Fargo customer service is listed above.
