Get your Social Security benefit statement (SSA-1099)

A Social Security 1099 is a tax form mailed each year in January to people who receive Social Security benefits. It shows the total amount of benefits you received last year.

If you have questions about your SSA-1099, call Social Security at 1-800-555-0160; for questions about your taxes, contact the IRS.

If you live outside the United States, contact your nearest U.S. embassy or consulate.
