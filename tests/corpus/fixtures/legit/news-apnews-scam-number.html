Retirees Report Fake Refund Calls

Police in three counties say they have taken dozens of reports this month from older residents who received unexpected calls about an order they never placed.

In each case the caller said he was with Amazon customer service and asked the resident to call back on 1-888-555-0177 to cancel the charge. Residents who called that number were then talked through installing software on their computers.

Amazon says customers can check any order by signing in to their account directly.
