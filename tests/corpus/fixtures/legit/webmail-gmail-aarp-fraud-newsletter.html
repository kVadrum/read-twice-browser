This week's scam alert: the "bank investigator" call

AARP Fraud Watch Network <fraudwatch@aarp.org>

Oct 20
to me

How the scam works

A caller says they are investigating fraud at your bank. To "protect" your savings, they insist on gift cards only, and they warn you: do not tell anyone, not even family.

What to do

Hang up. Call your bank yourself, using the number printed on the back of your card. Real banks never ask you to buy gift cards.
