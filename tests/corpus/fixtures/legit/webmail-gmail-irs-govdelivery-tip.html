IRS Tax Tip 2026-142

The IRS sends a letter when it needs to confirm a taxpayer's identity before processing a return. The letter explains what to do.

Taxpayers who received one can confirm their identity online at IRS.gov, or call the IRS identity verification line at 1-800-555-0184.

The IRS never starts contact by text message or social media.
